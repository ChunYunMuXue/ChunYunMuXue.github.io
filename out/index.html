About

Hello ☺️ I'm a student and a xcpcer / oier;

Since childhood, I have been enthusiastic about algorithmic problem solving and have devoted significant time to competitive programming, including OI and XCPC.

I am still exploring my long-term research interests.

And now I'm happy that I'm learning the Agentic RL and Learning Theory with Prof. JianQian.
